Project I
Pathogen Subversion of NF-κB Signalling via Effector Protein [X]

Using CRISPR screens and proximity-ligation proteomics to identify host binding partners of secreted effector [X] from [pathogen]. Initial data suggests a novel interaction with IκB kinase that dampens pro-inflammatory cytokine production during early infection.

Project II
Single-Cell Transcriptomics of Infected Macrophage Populations

scRNA-seq of primary human macrophages during [pathogen] infection at 2, 6, and 24 h post-infection. Identified a transcriptionally distinct "tolerised" macrophage subpopulation that is preferentially exploited for intracellular replication.

Project III
Interferon-Stimulated Gene Profiling During [Virus] Infection

Bulk RNA-seq time-course of [virus]-infected cells with and without IFN-β pre-treatment. Identified 12 ISGs significantly suppressed during active replication; three candidates validated by individual knockdown and plaque assay.
