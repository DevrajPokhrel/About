About

The researcher
behind the bench.

I am Devraj Pokhrel, a BSc Biotechnology graduate from Purbanchal University, Nepal, with a deep commitment to understanding and combating infectious disease. My research sits at the intersection of microbiology, molecular biology, and bioinformatics — I am equally comfortable running microbial assays in the lab and building automated NGS pipelines on a command line.

Currently I work as a Research Assistant at two institutions simultaneously: at the Nexus Institute of Research and Innovation (NIRI), I am leading a comprehensive One Health review of antifungal resistance in Nepal; at the Center of Health and Disease Studies (CHDS), I contribute to projects on dengue outbreaks, phytochemical antimicrobials, and mosquito vector surveillance.

My bioinformatics experience was sharpened during a remote internship at Dr. Omics Labs, New Delhi, where I engineered automated pipelines for DNA-seq, RNA-seq, metagenomics, and microarray analysis using tools such as GATK, STAR, QIIME2, and R/Bioconductor.

I am passionate about science mentorship and community outreach — I have mentored 11 undergraduate thesis students and 5 lab interns, and I regularly contribute Op-Eds on infectious disease and public health to Nepali newspapers. I am a graduate of the Aspire Leaders Program (Cohort 2, 2025) run by the Aspire Institute, USA.
