I decode how pathogens defeat
our defences — and how we fight back.

I am Devraj Pokhrel, a molecular biology and infectious disease researcher based in Kathmandu, Nepal. I hold a BSc in Biotechnology from Purbanchal University and currently work as a Research Assistant at two institutions — the Nexus Institute of Research and Innovation (NIRI) and the Center of Health and Disease Studies (CHDS).

My research centres on antimicrobial resistance (AMR), host–pathogen interactions, and functional genomics. I approach these questions from both the wet lab — microbial assays, molecular diagnostics, vector surveillance — and the command line, building automated NGS pipelines for DNA-seq, RNA-seq, and metagenomics analyses.

I am deeply committed to science mentorship and public health communication. I have mentored 11 undergraduate thesis students and regularly write Op-Eds on infectious disease for Nepali newspapers.
