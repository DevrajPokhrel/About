Devraj Pokhrel — molecular biologist & infectious disease researcher, Kathmandu, Nepal. BSc Biotechnology (Purbanchal University). Research Assistant at NIRI and CHDS.

My work focuses on antimicrobial resistance (AMR), host–pathogen interactions, and functional genomics. I combine wet-lab microbiology with NGS pipelines (DNA‑seq, RNA‑seq, metagenomics).

Mentor to 11 undergrad theses; writer on science and health policy.
